08

Eight principles that have shaped how I think about discovery, requirements, and building solutions. Not from a textbook. From real projects, real clients, and real trade-offs.

A note on all of this

These are not frameworks.
They are scar tissue.

Every one of these principles came from a moment where something went wrong, or almost went wrong, or went right for a reason I only understood afterwards. The value is not in knowing these ideas. It is in having lived them enough times to recognise the situations where they apply before the damage is done.
